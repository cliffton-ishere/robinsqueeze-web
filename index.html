Every routed buy.

Acquires GME.

A stock-paired liquidity protocol on Robinhood Chain. Scroll

A routed RSQ purchase splits your USDG in one transaction. You receive RSQ. The Pair Vault receives canonical GME Stock Tokens. Protocol-owned liquidity receives the remainder. The split is enforced by the contract, not calculated in this page — and no administrator holds your funds at any point.

Read this precisely. Only purchases executed through the official Paired Buy Router acquire GME. RSQ is an ordinary ERC-20 with no transfer tax, so anyone can trade it directly against a pool and bypass the router entirely. We say “every routed buy acquires GME” — never “every buy”.

Enforced on-chain

Where every dollar goes.

90%  RSQ to you
9000 bps — buys RSQ, paid straight to your wallet 800 bps — acquires GME, paid straight to the Pair Vault 200 bps — funds protocol-owned RSQ liquidity

The basis points are immutable constants in PairedBuyRouter and sum to exactly 10,000. The 2% slice is computed as the remainder rather than a third multiplication, so the three parts add up to your input exactly, at every size, with no rounding dust left in the router. There is no admin function that can change the split. The full allocation is shown to you before you sign.

Pair Vault

GME goes in. Nothing comes out.

The vault has no withdraw function and no admin transfer. Recovery of the pair asset is refused at the contract level, and the contract is not upgradeable.

What the vault holds, stated accurately. Robinhood Stock Tokens provide tokenized economic exposure. They are not direct legal ownership of shares in GameStop Corp., carry no shareholder or voting rights, and pay no dividend. The vault holds tokens; it does not hold equity.

Holding RSQ does not give you a claim on the vault's contents. No redemption right exists, and none is implied.

Liquidity reality. The canonical GME/USDG venue holds roughly 194,750 USDG. That is real, arbitraged liquidity, but it is thin enough that large routed buys move the price against themselves. Slippage limits are conservative by default and the router enforces a minimum output you set — it will revert rather than fill you at a bad price.

The contract enforces the split. Not the interface.

What this is not. The Pressure Score measures transparent Robinsqueeze protocol activity and nothing else. It is not a prediction, forecast, target or guarantee of any movement in GME, in GameStop Corp.'s publicly traded stock, or in RSQ.

It deliberately excludes short interest, options open interest, short-seller identities, coordinated trading data and price targets. Buying RSQ does not cause GameStop's underlying share price to move, and nothing here should be read as a claim that it does.
